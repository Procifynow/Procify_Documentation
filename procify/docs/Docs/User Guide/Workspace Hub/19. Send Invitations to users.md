# Workspace Hub — Send Invitations to users

**Step 1:** In **My Apps,** proceed to the **Roles & Users** section, **Edit** the screen and click on **Users**.

**Step 2:** A pop-up will appear showcasing the users. Select the users to send invitations.

**Step 3:** Click on the **Share Apk** button. A pop-up will appear. Click on the **OK** button.

Invitations will be sent to the email ID.
