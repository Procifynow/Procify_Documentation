# Workspace Hub — Assign Apps with App Authorisation

**Step 1:** Go to **App Authorisation** under the Menu headerApp Lifecycle.

**Step 2:** All the users of the portal will be listed on this page. Select your User.

You can also search for your user name using the search bar. Click to open the details section. 

**Step 3:** In the details section, you will find the list of Apps assigned to the particular user. 

**Step 4:** You can add any app that is required by clicking on the **Add** button. Make the screen **editable** first. 

**Step 5:** A list of apps will be displayed to you as a popup. Select the one that is required and click the add button.

The app will be added to your list. 

The user will be assigned to the app as manager and the app will be reflected in the Apps screen from where the remaining roles can be assigned to the required users.
