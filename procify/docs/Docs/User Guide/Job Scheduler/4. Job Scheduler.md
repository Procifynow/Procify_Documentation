# Job Scheduler

**Scheduled Tasks**, **Jobs**, and **Job Execution Log** together form the core components of a **Job Scheduler**, which automates, monitors, and logs job executions, ensuring that tasks are completed on time and according to predefined rules.

## Job definition

- A Job definition is a screen where the job’s templates are created, set/scheduled and kept systematically.

## Jobs

- A **Job** refers to a specific execution of a job. Each time a job is run, a new instance is created. It represents the occurrence of that job with its unique execution parameters, timestamps, status, and related metadata.

- A **Job** allows you to track the job's progress, its start and end time, and its outcome (whether successful or failed). Each Job is distinct and can be tracked individually.

## Job Execution Log

- A **Job Execution Log** contains detailed records or entries related to the execution of a Job. It provides a step-by-step log of events, actions, and messages generated during the execution of the job.

- **Job Execution Logs** are useful for debugging, auditing, and understanding the behavior of a job during its execution. They typically include information like warnings, errors, and system outputs that occurred while the Jobs was running.
