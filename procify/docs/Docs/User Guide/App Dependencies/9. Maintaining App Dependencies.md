# App Dependencies — Maintaining App Dependencies

Maintaining a proper hierarchy between app versions is crucial for accurate dependency management, for this a correct approach to establish a multi-level hierarchy is required, ensuring that dependencies are calculated correctly.

To maintain the hierarchy effectively, it always starts from **the top-most parent** and works your way down to the **bottom-most child.**

**Example**:

**Current Versions:**

- A-1 (Top-most Parent)

- B-1

- C-1

- D-1 (Bottom-most Child)

**Correct Hierarchy:** A-1 -\> B-1 -\> C-1 -\> D-1

**Note**: To ensure accurate dependency management;

- Always maintain hierarchy from the top-most parent to the bottom-most child.

- This method prevents errors in dependency calculations and keeps the app structure coherent.
